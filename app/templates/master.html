Heads are the categories in the grid and on the Expense and Revenue pages, arranged as a tree like the OPEX sheet: a head can have sub-heads, and a parent's figures in the grid include its sub-heads. For says where a head is offered: Expense heads on expenses, Revenue heads on revenue, Both on either. Sub-heads of an expense or revenue head are of the same kind. Order sets the order among heads at the same level; heads in use are deactivated, never deleted.
